Pallet storage in Reno: questions

Is there a minimum stay or pallet count?

No minimums. Store one pallet for a day or a truckload on a long-term contract. Storage is billed weekly.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Short-term and monthly storage need no contract. Long-term contract warehousing is there when you want a set block of space.

Can I release part of what I store?

Yes. Full or partial releases go to any carrier you send, onto an outbound truck at our dock, or out on our own trucks within 100 miles of Sparks.

What can't you store?

Anything that needs temperature control: we handle dry freight only. Call first for hazmat or other regulated freight and for single pieces over 4,500 lb.
