Why keep inventory in Reno

Reno sits on I-80, the main freight lane between Northern California and the rest of the country. Freight staged here reaches Sacramento the same morning and most of the western U.S. within one day.

Nevada has no corporate income tax, and its Freeport law exempts inventory stored here and shipped out of state from inventory tax.

How it works

A standard 3PL process from RFQ to proof of delivery, documented at every step.

  1. 1

    Request for quote

    Send your RFQ: commodity, pallet count, dimensions, turns per month and services. Written rate quote within one business day.

  2. 2

    Onboarding

    Service agreement, certificate of insurance and SKU list set up in our system. Integrations tested before go-live.

  3. 3

    Inbound and receiving

    Send an ASN and book a dock appointment. We receive against your ASN and BOL, photograph, and put away. OS&D reported same day.

  4. 4

    Order release and shipping

    Submit a release order. We pick, build and load, issue the BOL, record the seal and send confirmation with photos and POD.
