Pickups and deliveries in Reno: questions

How far do your trucks go from Reno?

Within 100 miles of our Sparks dock. For freight going further, we load the carrier you send, or cross-dock it onto an outbound truck.

Yes. Recurring pickups and deliveries run in the same agreed window each time, on the schedule you set.

Is there a minimum?

No minimums. A single pallet run is fine.

What freight do your trucks carry?

Dry freight: pallets, drums, crates and mixed freight. Call first for oversized pieces, anything over 4,500 lb, and hazmat or other regulated freight.
