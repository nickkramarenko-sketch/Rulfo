How rework works

You decide before we touch it. That's the rule at every dock.

  1. 1

    Found at the door

    A leaning, crushed or mixed pallet is photographed the moment it's unloaded and noted on the paperwork.

  2. 2

    You approve

    We tell you what it needs and what it costs. Nothing extra is charged until you say yes.

  3. 3

    Rebuild

    Re-palletized, restacked or sorted by SKU onto new pallets, with damaged cartons set aside and counted.

  4. 4

    Wrap and label

    Shrink wrap, corner boards and banding as the load needs, and new labels where the old ones are gone or wrong.

  5. 5

    Photos after

    Photographed again and released to the next truck, stored, or delivered on our own trucks.

What we fix

Freight that shifted in transit, pallets built badly at origin, mixed SKUs that a receiver will refuse. Rework turns a problem load into one that ships.

Because it's done at a cross-dock, the rebuilt freight can go straight back out on an outbound truck, often the same day it arrived.
