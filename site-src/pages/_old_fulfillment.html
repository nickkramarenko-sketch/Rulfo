Why sellers need a prep partner now

As of January 1, 2026, Amazon no longer preps or labels FBA inventory in the U.S. Every unit has to arrive at Amazon fully prepped and labeled by the seller or a prep center. That's the work we do.

Store your bulk inventory with us in Reno, and we send prepped cases into Amazon as your stock runs low, so you aren't paying Amazon's storage rates for months of inventory.

  • FNSKU labelingCovering old barcodes, one label per unit
  • Poly baggingSuffocation-warning bags for apparel and loose items
  • Bubble wrapFragile and glass items protected to spec
  • BundlingSets bagged or shrink-wrapped and marked as one unit
  • Case packingBoxed and labeled within Amazon's size and weight limits
  • InspectionDamaged or wrong units pulled and reported before shipping
