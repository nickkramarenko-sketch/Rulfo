Sending freight to us

Following these keeps receiving fast and your counts accurate. Not sure which dock? Compare locations.

Before it ships

  • Send the BOL or an advance shipment notice with quantities and PO numbers
  • Book the truck in the client portal, or by phone or email
  • Put your company name and reference number on the BOL

Freight

  • Dry freight only: standard pallets, drums, crates, oversized pieces, loose cargo and mixed loads
  • Standard 48" × 40" pallets, stretch-wrapped, where you can
  • Forklifts rated to 4,500 lb: call first for heavier pieces
  • Hazmat or other regulated freight: call first

The same day: counts and photos for every load in the client portal.
