How cross-docking works in Reno

East Coast consolidators send us one westbound trailer. We check the seal, unload, count against the BOL and photograph every load. Reno freight stays on our floor for delivery, California freight goes back on the same trailer in your delivery order, and anything that has to wait goes into storage.

Other lanes work the same way through our eight doors. Partial loads can wait on the floor for the next truck; pallets that stay are billed weekly as storage.

Why Reno for cross-docking

Our Sparks dock is on I‑80 east of Reno, on the way into Northern California from the east. Sacramento is about 2 hours west, the Bay Area about 4, Carson City about 1, and most of California, Oregon, Utah and Arizona is about a day's drive.

What freight can you cross-dock?

Dry freight: standard pallets, drums, crates, oversized pieces, loose cargo and mixed loads. Our forklifts are rated to 4,500 lb; call first for heavier pieces and for hazmat or other regulated freight.
