How consolidation works

Paying for five partial shipments when one truck would do costs money. We do the waiting for you.

  1. 1

    Plan the load

    Tell us what's coming in, from whom, and where the outbound truck goes, stop by stop.

  2. 2

    Receive

    Each inbound shipment is checked against its BOL or PO, counted and photographed at the door.

  3. 3

    Hold

    Pieces wait in one lane, never mixed with another customer's freight, until everything has arrived.

  4. 4

    Build

    Sorted by stop, palletized, wrapped and labeled so each receiver gets only its own freight.

  5. 5

    Release

    Counted and photographed once more, then released to your carrier, or delivered by our own trucks within the dock's radius.

Why consolidate at a cross-dock

Consolidation is cross-docking with patience. The same crew that turns trailers the same day knows how to stage freight for a week without losing a carton.

If a shipment is late, the rest waits safely. If one arrives damaged, you get photos the same day and decide what to do, so the outbound load leaves complete and clean.

  • InboundLTL pieces, partial pallets, several shippers or POs
  • OutboundOne truckload or a multi-stop load, sorted by stop
  • LabelsEach pallet labeled by stop, PO or receiver
  • ProofCounts and photos at receiving and again at release
  • WaitingHeld until the load is complete; long waits can move to storage, billed weekly
  • FixesDamaged pieces rewrapped or rebuilt with your approval. See freight rework

How long will you hold freight before the load is complete?

Until the load is complete. We agree on the hold and how it is billed before the first shipment arrives; if the wait runs long, the freight can move to storage by the day, week or month, billed weekly.

Which dock should I use for consolidation?

Consolidation is a specialty in Sacramento and Harrisburg, and we do it in Reno–Sparks too. Pick the dock closest to where the outbound load is going.

Can you pick up the inbound pieces?

Yes, on our own trucks, within 150 miles of Sacramento and Harrisburg and 100 miles of Reno–Sparks. See pickups and deliveries.

What if one shipment arrives damaged?

We photograph it, tell you the same day and hold it until you decide. Rework or rewrapping is quoted first and nothing extra is charged until you approve it.
