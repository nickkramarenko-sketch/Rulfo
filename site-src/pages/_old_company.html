About 24 HR Crossdock

Since 2015 we have built our business unloading, splitting and reloading trailers for brokers, carriers and consolidators moving freight across the country. That work taught us what shippers care about most: freight counted right, handled carefully and never lost track of.

As our customers grew, we grew with them: a 40,000 sq ft dock in Reno–Sparks on I-80, a 40,000 sq ft dock in North Highlands outside Sacramento, and a 50-door dock in Middletown near Harrisburg. Same crew mindset and the same procedures at every door.

We still run it the way we started: the phone gets answered, trucks get turned fast, and when a customer asks for something unusual, the answer is usually yes.

Our operating standards

These are the procedures our crew follows on every shift. They're how we keep counts accurate and freight safe.

Receiving

  • Drivers check in at our kiosk, in 6 languages
  • Unloaded within 2 hours of check-in, during dock hours
  • Seal number checked against paperwork
  • Photos before unloading
  • Every pallet counted against the BOL or inbound notice
  • Overages, shortages and damage reported the same day

Storage

  • Every pallet gets a location in our system
  • One customer load per lane, never mixed
  • Regular inventory counts
  • Aging tracked so nothing gets forgotten

Shipping

  • Pallets pulled and recounted before loading
  • Photos of the load before doors close
  • Seal recorded on departure
  • Securement checked, photographed and signed before it leaves
  • Signed paperwork kept on file

Damage and exceptions

  • Photographed at the moment it's found, with a price to fix
  • You're notified right away
  • No restack or rework without your approval
  • Labor itemized on your invoice
