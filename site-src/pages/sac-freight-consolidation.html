Why Sacramento for freight consolidation

Northern California freight is spread out: vendors in the Bay Area, containers at Oakland and Stockton, plants around Sacramento. North Highlands sits between them, with I-80 and I-5 at the door, so the pieces come to one dock and one truck leaves full.

The finished load can head east on I-80 toward our Reno–Sparks dock (about 2 hours) and beyond, or run north and south on I-5. One trailer instead of several LTL shipments means fewer hand-offs on the long leg.

  • Freight brokersLTL pieces from several shippers built into one truckload you can cover
  • DistributorsOrders from several vendors combined for one receiver
  • ImportersContainer freight from Oakland or Stockton combined with domestic pallets
  • ManufacturersPlant output held until a full outbound load is ready

Sacramento consolidation questions

Planning a load? Call {PHONE} and we will map it out with you.

How long can freight wait for the rest of the load?

As long as it needs to. Short holds wait on the floor; longer ones move into storage, billed by the day, week or month, with no minimums.

Can you pick up the pieces for me?

Yes. Our own trucks pick up within 150 miles of North Highlands, one-time or on a recurring schedule, so a Sacramento-area shipper does not need a separate carrier to reach us.

What if one shipment arrives damaged?

It is photographed on arrival and you hear about it that day. If you want it rebuilt before it joins the load, we price the fix first and charge nothing extra until you say yes.

Who hauls the finished load?

Your carrier. We release the complete load to whoever you send, with the count and photos on file. For a final stop within 150 miles, our trucks can take it.
