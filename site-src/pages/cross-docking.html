Is it really reloaded the same day?

Yes, when the outbound truck is here the same day. If it isn't, the freight waits on the floor in its own lane until it is, and longer stays move to storage, billed weekly.

What happens if freight arrives short or damaged?

It's photographed at the door, noted on the paperwork and reported to you the same day. We can rebuild or rewrap it, but nothing extra is charged until you approve it.

What freight can you cross-dock?

Dry freight only: standard pallets, drums, crates, oversized pieces, loose cargo and mixed loads. Our forklifts are rated to 4,500 lb, so call first for heavier pieces, and call first for hazmat or other regulated freight.
