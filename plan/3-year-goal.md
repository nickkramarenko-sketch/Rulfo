# 3-Year Goal: $10M Warehouse Revenue

**Goal:** $10 million a year in warehouse revenue (cross-dock, transload, storage, value-add) by **end of 2029**, from 2 sites: **Reno/Sparks NV** + **Chicago**.
**Start:** Reno at ~1,200 pallets a week.

All prices below use the draft rate sheet (cross-dock $12/pallet, storage $19/pallet/month + $6 in + $6 out).

---

## The math

| | Pallets | Revenue a year |
|---|---|---|
| Today (start) | 1,200 cross-dock pallets/week | ~$750k |
| Goal | — | $10M (13× today) |

**Cross-dock alone can't get there.** $10M at $12/pallet is 16,000 pallets a week, about 600 trucks a week. Too much labor for the money.

**Storage is the engine.** A pallet sitting in storage pays every month without being touched. One storage spot earns about **$300 a year** (storage + in/out fees). 10,000 spots = ~$3M a year per building.

**The mix that hits $10M:**

| Per site | Reno | Chicago |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-dock / transload | 3,000 pallets/week → ~$1.9M | 3,000 pallets/week → ~$1.9M |
| Storage | 10,000 pallet spots full → ~$3M | 10,000 pallet spots full → ~$3M |
| Value-add (labels, wrap, kitting, FBA prep, containers) | ~$0.2M | ~$0.2M |
| **Total** | **~$5M** | **~$5M** |

Space needed: each site needs about **120,000–150,000 sq ft with racking**. The current Sparks unit will not hold 10,000 pallets, so a bigger Reno building comes in Year 2.

| **Year 3 (2029)** | 3,000 pallets/wk + 10,000 storage | 3,000 pallets/wk + 10,000 storage | **~$10M** |

## What has to be true
1. **Long-term storage customers**: importers, CA manufacturers, retail brands (see `marketing/ideal-customer-profile.md`). About 30–50 customers storing 200–500 pallets each per site.
2. **Sales team**: salesperson #1 in 2027, #2 when Chicago opens. Plus cold email, Google, listings.
3. **Cookie-cutter playbook**: the SOPs, QuickBooks setup, portal and KPI sheet must work in Reno first, then get copied to Chicago as-is.
4. **A manager per site** so Nick runs the business, not the dock.
5. **People**: ~50–65 staff total at $10M (3PLs do about $150–200k revenue per employee).
6. **Systems**: a real warehouse system (WMS) once storage passes ~2,000 pallets.
7. **Money**: racking, forklifts, leases and deposits for 2 big buildings (budget to be built with investors).

## Why Chicago
Biggest rail hub in the US. Containers from LA/LB and Seattle come in by train (Joliet/Elwood, Rochelle ramps) and need transload and storage. Same playbook as Reno: unload, store, ship.

## Check every quarter
- Pallets/week cross-dock
- Storage spots full (and % of building full)
- Revenue run-rate vs this plan
- Number of storage customers
- Damage rate, on-time %
