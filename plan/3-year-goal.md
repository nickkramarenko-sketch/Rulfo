# 3-Year Goal: $10M Warehouse Revenue, 4 Locations

**Goal:** $10 million a year in warehouse revenue (cross-dock, transload, storage, value-add) by **end of 2029**, across 4 sites:
- **Reno/Sparks, NV**: today, ~1,200 pallets a week to start
- **North Highlands / Sacramento, CA**: today
- **Middletown, PA**: today
- **Chicago, IL**: new

That's **~$2.5M per site**.

All prices below use the draft rate sheet (cross-dock $12/pallet, storage $19/pallet/month + $6 in + $6 out). CA and PA may price a little different once local rates are checked.

---

## The math

| | Revenue a year |
|---|---|
| Reno today (1,200 cross-dock pallets/week) | ~$750k |
| Goal, all 4 sites | $10M |

**Cross-dock alone can't get there.** $10M at $12/pallet is 16,000 pallets a week, about 600 trucks across all sites. Too much labor for the money.

**Storage is the engine.** A pallet sitting in storage pays every month without being touched: about **$300 a year per storage spot** (storage + in/out fees).

**One site at $2.5M (the cookie-cutter target):**

| | Volume | Revenue a year |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-dock / transload | 1,500 pallets/week | ~$0.95M |
| Storage | 5,000 pallet spots full | ~$1.5M |
| Value-add (labels, wrap, kitting, FBA prep, containers) | | ~$0.1M |
| **Total per site** | | **~$2.5M** |

**Space per site: ~60,000–75,000 sq ft with racking.** Check whether each current building can hold 5,000 racked pallets. If not, that site moves or adds space in Year 2.
**People per site: ~12–16** (one site manager, shift leads, forklift drivers, office/billing). About 50–65 total.

## The big selling point: a coast-to-coast network
- West: **Sacramento + Reno**
- Middle: **Chicago**
- East: **Pennsylvania**

One company, one rate sheet, one portal, every pallet photographed, covering most of the US within about 2 days by truck. Importers and brands with customers nationwide will pay more for that than for 4 separate warehouses. Sell it as a network once Chicago is open.

## What has to be true
1. **Long-term storage customers:** importers, manufacturers, retail brands (see `marketing/ideal-customer-profile.md`). About 15–25 storage customers per site, at 200–500 pallets each.
2. **Cookie-cutter playbook:** SOPs, QuickBooks setup, portal, rate sheet and KPI sheet perfected in Reno, then copied to CA, PA and Chicago **as-is**. That's why Prep comes first.
3. **A manager per site.** Nick runs the network, not a dock.
4. **Sales:** salesperson #1 in 2027 (West), #2 in 2028 (East + Chicago). Plus cold email, Google and listings for every site.
5. **Systems:** a real warehouse system (WMS) once total storage passes ~2,000 pallets. All 4 sites on the same one.
6. **Money:** racking, forklifts, leases and deposits (budget to be built with investors).

## Check every quarter, per site
- Pallets/week cross-dock
- Storage spots full (and % of building full)
- Revenue run-rate vs this plan
- Number of storage customers
- Damage rate, on-time %
