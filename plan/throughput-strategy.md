# Throughput First: How to Scale All 4 Sites at Once

**The idea:** pallet in, pallet out is the business. Storage and value-add only happen once the pallets are already here, so they're the bonus, not the engine.
**Urgency:** all 4 sites (Reno, CA, PA, Chicago) grow at the same time. 3 years to $10M.

---

## The math (throughput only, no storage needed)

| | Pallets a month | Revenue a year |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity, 4 sites | 120,000 | ~$17M at $12/pallet |
| **NVK volume** | **43,000** (~10,750 per site, ~2,500 per site a week) | $4.1M at $8 · $5.2M at $10 · $6.2M at $12 |
| Needed for $10M (at ~$11 average) | ~76,000 | $10M |
| **Gap to sell** | **~33,000** (~8,250 per site a month, ~1,900 a week) | ~$4–6M |

- NVK alone fills **36%** of capacity and gets roughly half way to $10M.
- $10M with **throughput only** = **~63%** of capacity. Storage and value-add are pure upside on top.
- **Lock NVK in writing**: volume, rate per pallet, 12+ month term. It's the anchor that gets the other 3 sites moving on day one.
- **Risk:** no single customer should be over ~50% of revenue by Year 3. If NVK slows down, the sites need other volume.

## The client profile (throughput customer)

**Must have**
- Ships **2+ trucks (~50+ pallets) a week** through at least one of our 4 markets. **Best: needs 2 or more of our markets.**
- **Recurring lanes**, 12+ months, not one-off spot loads.
- Dry, palletized, standard 48×40, non-hazmat.
- Needs fast turns: same day or next day.
- Pays Net 30, passes a credit check.

**Who to talk to**
- Shippers: VP / Director of Transportation, Logistics Manager, Network Planning
- Brokers and freight networks: Carrier / Partner Network Manager, Cross-dock or LTL Operations, Procurement
- Carriers: Linehaul Manager, Terminal Operations

**Size**
- Shippers: $50M+ revenue
- Brokers: $100M+ revenue
- Carriers: 50+ trucks

**Buying signals**
- Annual RFP season (Q4–Q1: **right now**)
- New DC or Amazon building in our markets
- Retail vendor programs (Walmart, Target, Costco, Amazon)
- Port or rail volume shifting
- Peak season overflow (Aug–Dec)

**Red flags**
- Spot loads only
- Hazmat or temperature control
- Slow payer
- Wants to pay under our floor price

## Who sends the most pallets (ranked for throughput)

1. **Freight networks that use partner cross-docks.** They need docks in many cities and plug local warehouses into their network. Examples: WARP (~60 partner cross-docks), OLIMP / DAT cross-docking, and big brokers' LTL / consolidation teams (C.H. Robinson, TQL, RXO, Arrive, Echo, Uber Freight). **One contract can feed all 4 sites.**
2. **Retail cross-dock and pool distribution.** Brands shipping to Walmart, Target and Costco DCs: one full truck in, split into store and DC deliveries out. All 4 of our markets have big retail DCs.
3. **Amazon / retail consolidation.** Many small shippers' pallets combined into full trucks with one dock appointment at Amazon or retail DCs. Every one of our markets has Amazon buildings.
4. **Import transload.**
   - Oakland → Reno and Sacramento
   - NY/NJ and Baltimore → PA
   - Rail ramps → Chicago
5. **LTL and regional carriers** with no terminal of their own in one of our markets. We act as their dock (agent / interline).
6. **Manufacturers** with steady outbound: Reno (Tesla and its suppliers), PA, Chicago.

## Look like the company we want to be
- **Sell the network, not a warehouse:** "4 locations, coast to coast, capacity to move 120,000 pallets a month."
- **Map** on the website and one-pager: Sacramento, Reno, Chicago, PA.
- One phone number and one quote inbox for all 4 sites, answered 24/7.
- A **city page for each site × each service** (cross-dock, transload, storage), like OLIMP.
- A **case study**: "43,000 pallets a month across 4 sites for a national carrier." Use NVK, only with their OK and without naming them if they prefer.
- **Trade shows where buyers are:**
  - TIA Capital Ideas (freight brokers)
  - SMC3 (LTL carriers)
  - Manifest (Las Vegas, tech/freight networks)
  - TPM (Long Beach, importers)
  - IWLA (warehouses)
