## 3. Setup checklist (weeks 1–3)

Nothing here gets bought or sent without Nick's OK.

1. **Domains.** Buy 3 look-alike domains (e.g. `24hrcrossdockreno.com`, `get24hrcrossdock.com`, `24hrcrossdock-nv.com`). Never cold email from the main domain.
2. **Inboxes.** 2 Google Workspace inboxes per domain (nick@, sales@). Set SPF, DKIM, DMARC. Forward replies to the main inbox.
3. **Warm-up.** Connect the inboxes to Instantly. Let them warm up 2–3 weeks before sending.
4. **Apollo.** 1 seat. Build the lists below. Export to Instantly, or send from Apollo directly if its deliverability is good.
5. **HubSpot (free).** Pipeline stages: New lead → Qualified → Call booked → Quote sent → Won / Lost. Connect the website quote form.
6. **Quote form filter.** Fewer than 10 pallets and only storage → polite auto-reply, no call.
7. **Speed.** Text alert to Nick for every qualified lead. Goal: call back within 1 hour.
8. **Every email** includes the business mailing address and an opt-out line (CAN-SPAM).

Send limits: 30 emails per inbox per day max → 6 inboxes ≈ 180 a day ≈ 4,000 a month.

## 4. Who to target (Apollo filters)

### List A: Freight forwarders and customs brokers (top priority)
- Keywords: freight forwarder, NVOCC, customs broker, international logistics
- Location (HQ or branch): California, Nevada, plus Chicago and New Jersey forwarders with West Coast imports
- Size: 5–500 employees
- Titles: Owner, President, Branch Manager, Operations Manager, Import Manager

### List B: Freight brokers (top priority)
- Keywords: freight brokerage, truckload broker, LTL broker
- Location: anywhere in the US (they move freight through Reno)
- Size: 5–1,000 employees
- Titles: Owner, VP Operations, Operations Manager, Carrier Sales Manager, Capacity Manager

### List C: California manufacturers and brands (contract storage)
- Industries: consumer goods, food and beverage, furniture, building materials, e-commerce, wholesale
- Location: Bay Area, Sacramento, Central Valley
- Size: 20–1,000 employees
- Titles: Supply Chain Manager, Logistics Manager, Director of Operations, Warehouse Manager, COO

### List D: Importers (transload + container unloading)
- Source: ImportYeti (free). Search consignees receiving containers through Oakland and Long Beach that ship to Nevada or inland California.
- Find the people in Apollo. Titles: Import Manager, Logistics Manager, Owner

### List E: Amazon sellers (FBA prep, lower priority)
- Keywords: Amazon seller, e-commerce brand, DTC
- Size: 5–200 employees
- Titles: Founder, Head of Operations

### Cross-dock carriers (phone + Russian channels, not Apollo)
- Apollo has few small carriers. Use FMCSA carrier data: 5–50 power units, based in IL, TN, GA, NC and the Sacramento area.
- Post in Russian-language Telegram and Facebook trucking groups. Send them to the Russian landing page.
- Call them. Carriers answer phones more than email.

## 6. Nick's weekly routine (about 10–15 hours)

- **Every morning, 30 min:** answer replies, book calls.
- **Every weekday, 1–2pm:** sales calls and follow-up calls from the sequences.
- **New lead alert:** call back within 1 hour.
- **Monday, 15 min:** update the scoreboard.
