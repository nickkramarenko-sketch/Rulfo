## Rules for Nick
- **Floor:** never quote cross-dock under $10/pallet or $225/truck, or storage under $16/pallet/month, without checking labor cost.
- **Volume pricing:** 5+ trucks/week or 200+ pallets stored → up to 10–15% off. Ask for a 3-month commitment in return.
- **Always charge for:** floor-loaded work, re-wrap, labor over 30 minutes, storage past free time.
- **Give away free:** photos, counts, BOL/POD, damage report. That's the selling point.
- Review prices every 6 months against new Reno quotes.
