# Ideal Customer Profile: 24 HR Crossdock, Reno

Based on the Reno freight analysis (Oct 2026). Use this to build Apollo lists, decide who to call first, and say no to bad fits.

## The ideal customer in one line
A company that moves **full pallets** through or into Northern Nevada **every week**, needs a dock that answers fast, and pays **Net 30 or better**.

## Must-haves (all 5)
1. Freight is on pallets or in crates (no loose piece-picking).
2. Repeat volume: 2+ trucks a week, or 100+ pallets stored.
3. Freight goes into or through Reno / Northern Nevada / inland West.
4. We can reach the person who picks the warehouse (titles below).
5. Pays on time (Net 30 or better, or card for the first 3 loads).

## Profile details

### 1. Forwarders + customs brokers (List A)
- **Titles:** Owner, Branch Manager, Operations Manager, Import Manager
- **Where:** California and Nevada offices; Chicago and New Jersey forwarders with West Coast imports
- **Buying signals:** hiring import coordinators, new trade lane posts, tariff deadline rush (July 2026 Oakland imports +31%)
- **Red flags:** only air freight; only parcel

### 2. California manufacturers + brands (List C)
- **Industries:** consumer goods, beverages (packaged, dry), furniture, building materials, wholesale, e-commerce brands with bulk stock
- **Where:** Bay Area, Sacramento, Central Valley
- **Titles:** Supply Chain Manager, Logistics Manager, Director of Operations, COO
- **Buying signals:** job posts for warehouse staff, new product launch, lease ending, seasonal peak coming, tariff pull-forward
- **Red flags:** needs cold storage; needs AIB food-grade certification; under 50 pallets

### 3. Freight brokers (List B)
- **Titles:** Owner, VP Operations, Carrier Sales Manager, Capacity Manager
- **Where:** anywhere in the US
- **Buying signals:** posting Reno or I-80 loads on load boards, West Coast lanes
- **Red flags:** slow payers (check credit; card or quick-pay for the first 3 loads)

### 4. Importers (List D)
- **Source:** ImportYeti consignees landing at Oakland or Long Beach and shipping to Nevada / inland California
- **Titles:** Import Manager, Logistics Manager, Owner
- **Best fit:** palletized containers. Floor-loaded only at a price that pays for the labor.
- **Red flags:** containers go straight to their own DC by rail

### 5. Drayage carriers (List I)
- See `reno-outbound-playbook.md` section 4.

### 6. Solar (List F)
- **Apollo:** keywords "solar EPC", "utility-scale solar", "solar distribution", "energy storage"; Location: CA, NV, AZ, UT; Size 20–5,000
- **Titles:** Project Manager, Logistics Manager, Procurement Manager, Materials Manager, Site Logistics
- **Known projects nearby:** Sierra Solar (400 MW, Churchill County, due 2027), Libra Solar (700 MW, Yerington, end 2027)
- **Need before pitching:** $5M liability, cargo cover, cameras, written photo-receiving SOP
- **Red flags:** battery containers (BESS) are not pallets; Li-ion = hazmat

### 7. Data-center build (List G)
- **Apollo:** keywords "data center construction", "mission critical", "electrical contractor", "critical power"; Location: NV, CA, AZ, TX HQs; Size 50+
- **Titles:** Project Manager, Logistics Coordinator, Procurement Manager, Field Operations Manager
- **Projects:** Tahoe Reno Industrial Center (Switch, Apple, Tract/Fleet $10B, 400 MW)
- **Need before pitching:** same as solar, plus caged area and serialized receiving
- **Red flags:** oversize transformers and chillers (need a crane and yard)

### 8. Mining (List H)
- **Apollo:** keywords "mining supply", "mining equipment", "MRO", "industrial supply"; Location: NV, UT, ID, AZ; Size 20–5,000
- **Titles:** Purchasing Manager, Procurement, Warehouse Manager, Logistics Manager
- **Where freight goes:** Winnemucca, Elko, Thacker Pass (I-80 east)
- **Red flags:** explosives and chemicals; mine already runs its own Reno warehouse

## Say no (bad fit)
- Under 10 pallets, storage only, one-time
- Lithium cells or battery packs (Class 9 hazmat)
- Food needing AIB / BRCGS certification or temperature control
- Unit-level piece-picking (FBA prep, e-commerce orders) as the main job
- Floor-loaded containers at palletized prices
- Customers who won't give a certificate of insurance or pay terms
