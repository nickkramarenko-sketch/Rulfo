# Copy Rubric (auto-improve loop)

Grade every email 0–100. Rewrite and re-grade until it scores **85+**. Stop after 5 rounds and show Nick the best version.

| # | Check | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Under 90 words (email 1), under 60 (follow-ups) | 10 |
| 2 | Names a pain this customer type really has (from `customer-playbooks.md`), in their words | 15 |
| 3 | One clear, low-effort ask (a yes/no question, not a meeting) | 15 |
| 4 | Sounds like one person typing, not marketing | 10 |
| 5 | First line is specific to them and true (or the list-level line) | 10 |
| 6 | Subject: 2–5 words, plain, no hype, no clickbait | 10 |
| 7 | No links, images or attachments in email 1 | 5 |
| 8 | No spam words (free, guarantee, act now, %, $$$, !!!) | 5 |
| 9 | No prices, no customer names | 5 |
| 10 | One idea per email; follow-ups add something new | 10 |
| 11 | Signature has address and opt-out line | 5 |

Fail automatically (score 0): invented facts, a price, a customer name, or a fake "re:" on email 1.

## Reverse lead magnet (email 2 option)
Offer something useful instead of a pitch:
- CA shippers: "Want a quick Bay Area vs Reno storage cost check for your pallet count?"
- Importers: "Want a snapshot of how many pallets your containers turned into last year?"
- Brokers: "Want our one-page Reno receiving guide (hours, appointments, Donner closures)?"
