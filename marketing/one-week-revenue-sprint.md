# One-Week Revenue Sprint

Goal: more money this week from customers you already have, plus new trucks from calls. About **2 hours a day** on top of running the warehouse.
Nothing gets sent, bought or posted without your OK. Claude writes everything; you send and call.

**Every day, 8–9am (dispatchers are in early):** 20 calls. Write each one in the tracker at the bottom.

---

## Day 1 (Tue): get ready
- [ ] **Approve the rate sheet** with Claude, 15 min. It's needed for every call this week.
- [ ] **Send the New Legend email** (draft is in Gmail) and call Sunny: "Save my number for any shifted load."
- [ ] **Google Business Profile:** Google Maps → 24 HR Crossdock Reno → "Own this business?" → request access.
- [ ] **Past customer list:** QuickBooks → Customers → write down everyone who hasn't booked in 60+ days. Or ask Claude to pull it.

## Day 2 (Wed): money from people who already know you
- [ ] **Call or text every past customer** (script A).
- [ ] **Chase overdue invoices.** QuickBooks → Invoices → Overdue → call each one. Cash today is revenue too.
- [ ] **Ask your 10 best customers for a Google review** (script B).

## Day 3 (Thu): drayage
- [ ] **20 calls to drayage companies** in Oakland and Sacramento (script C). Claude builds the list.
- [ ] Email the rate sheet to everyone who says yes, the same day.
- [ ] **Free listings** (30 min): Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, Warehouse Exchange, Waredock, Thomasnet.

## Day 4 (Fri): brokers + real estate
- [ ] **20 calls to freight brokers** in Reno and Sacramento (script C, say "brokers" instead of "drivers").
- [ ] **Email 5 Reno industrial real estate brokers** (CBRE, Colliers, NAI Alliance, Kidder Mathews, Dickson). Pitch: "When a tenant needs short-term space you can't fill, send them to us. We pay a referral fee."
- [ ] **Drop in at 5 offices** near Reno airport and in Sparks: donuts and the rate sheet. 10 minutes each.

## Day 5 (Mon, busy day): close + raise prices
- [ ] **Follow up everyone who said "maybe"** or got the rate sheet: "Any loads this week?"
- [ ] **Price check:** look at every regular customer in QuickBooks. Anyone under $10/pallet cross-dock, $225/truck or $16/pallet/month storage gets the new rate starting next month (script D).
- [ ] **Call EDAWN** (Reno economic development, edawn.org): ask how to join and get referred to companies moving to Reno.
- [ ] **Score the week** in the tracker.

---

## Scripts

**A. Past customer (call or text)**
> Hey [name], it's Nick from 24 HR Crossdock in Sparks. We've got dock space and pallet storage open right now, and we're open 24/7. Anything coming through Reno in the next couple weeks I can help with?

**B. Review request (text)**
> Hey [name], thanks for working with us. Would you leave us a quick Google review? It really helps a small business. [review link]. Thank you!

Get the review link: Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews" → copy link.

**C. Drayage / broker call (30 seconds)**
> Hi, this is Nick with 24 HR Crossdock in Sparks, Nevada, right on I-80. Who handles your Reno freight?
> [Get the right person.]
> We do cross-dock, transload and pallet storage, open 24/7, no after-hours fees. When a load shifts or a receiver in Reno can't take it, send it to us and your driver turns right around. Can I email you our rate sheet?
> [Yes → get email, send it today. No → "Can I check back in a month?"]

**D. Price update (email to regular customers under the floor)**
> Hi [name], thanks for being a regular customer. Starting [1st of next month], your rate for [service] will be [new rate]. Everything else stays the same: 24/7 dock, photos of every pallet, same-day reports. Any questions, call me directly at 916-616-3481.
