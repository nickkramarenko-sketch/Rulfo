How we handle your freight

  1. Before arrival. We review the manifest and outbound build plan as soon as the truck leaves your customer's facility.
  2. Receiving. Driver check-in, seal and count checked against the BOL, photos, and a scan on every carton.
  3. Build. Inbound pallets broken down and rebuilt by route per your plan and stacking order.
  4. Staging. Outbound pallets staged by route and ready for pickup.
  5. Pickup. Forklift loading at ground level. Your delivery pros scan each item at pickup.
  6. Damages and returns. Reported the same day with photos and held for LTL pickup.

A general outline. We will fine-tune the details together on the discovery call.
