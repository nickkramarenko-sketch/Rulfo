# What's new in the portal

Everything you had before is still there. These are the new parts, screen by screen.

## Customer portal

- **Book any time.** Customers can pick any minute, not only :00 and :30. Times are dock time (Pacific).
- **How busy is the day.** A small chart shows how busy each 2 hours is. It only shows counts. Other companies' names stay private.
- **More about each load.** Weight, pieces, "can it stack" and hazmat. Customers can also set the unload order.
- **Driver's language and how we track the truck.** Driver link, Samsara link, other GPS link, "I'll update it myself" or nothing.
- **After booking:** the truck's 4-digit check-in code and the driver link, with a Copy button. It shows "Waiting for our OK" until you confirm the time.
- **Truck page:** where the truck is now (booked, on the way, checked in, at the door, working, done), the wait clock, place in line and your 2-hour promise. Customers can update the ETA, change the time, see the counts against the BOL, the photos, the securement report and a list of everything that happened.
- **Schedule tab:** "Your trucks today" (on the way, at the dock or done), a short "done" note per truck, and "This week so far".
- **Damage tab:** photos, what happened, the fix price, Approve or Decline, and damage by month. Customers can set "auto-approve under $__".
- **Inventory tab:** "Our gear with you" (plywood, load bars, straps, tags) and the securement plan with an "I'm interested" button.
- **Alerts and settings** (gear button at the top): who should get a text or email for each event, and the Friday summary on or off.

## Driver kiosk (tablet by the door)

- Opens from Stations > Driver check-in (or the `#kiosk` link) and stays on the kiosk after a reload.
- Language first: English, Español, Русский, Українська, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, हिन्दी.
- Find the truck by company name or by the 4-digit check-in code, or walk in with no booking.
- The driver checks name and cell, takes a photo of the paperwork, and sees "Go to door D3" or "You are #2 in line, about 25 min".
- It goes back to the start by itself. To leave the kiosk, tap Staff and type an office name and PIN.

## Driver ETA link

- The link from the booking (it ends in `#eta` and the code) opens a simple page in the driver's language.
- Big buttons: 15 min, 30 min, 1 hour, 2 hours, or pick a time. A "running late" reason and a live location link are optional.
- It also shows the address, the hours and the dock phone number.

## Forklift crew

- Wait clocks on every truck, green / amber / red against the wait goal. The bell warns when a truck waits too long.
- The door picker shows who is at each busy door.
- A count that does not match the BOL needs a short note.
- **Damage report:** at least 2 photos, what kind of damage, and suggested fix prices for the customer.
- **Secure the load** (before a loaded truck leaves): our gear or the driver's, tag numbers, seal, 5 photos, how solid it is, and a signature. "Send to dispatch" waits until this is done.
- **Gear tab:** what is out with each customer, and "Log gear back" when a driver returns it.
- Points for securing a load on the leaderboard. New words in Spanish and Russian.

## Office

- **New tabs:** Yard, Outbox and Gear. On a phone the tab bar scrolls sideways.
- **Yard:** in the yard, at doors, done today, average wait, longest wait, promise kept. Then the trucks coming today, waiting (#1, #2 ...), at the doors and done.
- **Truck page extras:** check-in code and driver link (Copy), ETA and "Set ETA", "Confirm time", "Check in now" (same as Mark arrived, and it notes the office did it), tracking, driver's language, paperwork photos, this truck's messages and the securement report.
- **Bell:** customer bookings you have not confirmed yet. The bell list is now easy to read on the light look (before, it was white text on white).
- **Outbox:** every text and email the system would send, with filters, plus Friday summaries (preview, then "Queue Friday summaries").
- **Gear:** our gear out per customer, "Log gear back", and the customers who tapped "I'm interested".
- **Setup:** your promise (2 hours), the wait goal (30 min), the replacement pallet price and the securement plan price. Also the "Logins card on the sign-in page" switch: the test list that opens accounts without a PIN is now **off** unless you tap "Show it".
- **Crew tab:** phone, shift and rank for each forklift driver.

## TV

- A door turns red when its truck is past your promise.
- The yard list is in check-in order (#1, #2 ...) with clocks in green, amber or red.
- It fits on a phone too. To leave the TV, double-click and type an office PIN.

## Not live yet (please read)

- **Texts and emails are not connected.** Nothing is sent by itself. The Outbox shows exactly what would go out; send anything urgent by hand. The kiosk tells drivers "We'll text your door number", so text them from the Outbox or wave them in.
- **Customers' data stays in their own browser.** On the public link, what a customer does (bookings, ETA updates, alert choices, approvals, "I'm interested") stays on their device and does not reach you. They also do not see your trucks, counts, photos or damage reports from the dock.
- **The driver ETA link on a driver's own phone** cannot save the ETA. It makes a short text for the driver to send to the dock.
- **Shared data** (office, crew phones, kiosk and TV all on one board) only works in the private staff copy with shared data switched on. On this page every phone and tablet keeps its own copy; staff screens say "Saved on this device only".

## How to undo

Open the portal artifact on claude.ai, open its version history and pick the previous version. That puts the old portal back for everyone.
