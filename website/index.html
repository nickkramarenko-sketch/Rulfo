FAQ

Common questions

What is cross-docking?

Cross-docking means we unload your freight from an inbound truck, sort it, and load it onto outbound trucks with little or no storage time in between. It cuts warehouse costs and speeds up delivery.

Are you really open 24 hours?

Yes. Our dock runs around the clock so drivers can unload when they arrive. Call ahead or book an appointment for the fastest turn.

Do you have a monthly minimum?

No. You pay per pallet for what you actually move or store. No setup fees and no long-term contract required.

Do you offer short-term and long-term pallet storage?

Yes. Store pallets for a day, a week, or months. We offer daily, weekly and monthly rates for overflow, seasonal and long-term storage.

Can you handle floor-loaded containers?

Yes. We devan floor-loaded containers and trailers, palletize, shrink wrap and ship out.

What areas do you serve from Reno?

Reno, Sparks, the Tahoe Reno Industrial Center (TRIC), Carson City, Lake Tahoe and Northern California, plus outbound freight across the Western U.S.
