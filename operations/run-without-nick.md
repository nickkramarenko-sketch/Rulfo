# Run Without Nick: To-Do List + SOPs

Goal: the warehouse runs 2–3 days a week without Nick on site. The crew invoices, loads, communicates and cleans the same way every day, and Nick only handles the exceptions.

Monday and Tuesday are the busiest days. Nick's days off should be **Wednesday–Friday** to start.

## SOP 1: Invoicing (every job, before the truck leaves)
1. Open QuickBooks app → **+ New** → **Invoice**.
2. Customer: pick from the list. New customer: add company name, email, phone.
3. Add the service from the list (cross-dock, restack, Reno delivery $300, storage…). Never type a price by hand.
4. Quantity: number of pallets, hours or trucks.
5. In the message/memo: trailer number, load or PO number, pallet count, date.
6. Attach 2–4 photos: freight before and after, plus the BOL.
7. **Send** to the customer's email before the driver leaves.
8. Post in the group chat: "Invoiced [customer], [amount], [trailer #]".
- Customer pays on the spot (Zelle, card, check code): mark the invoice paid and write how they paid.
- Not sure about a price: text the shift lead. Don't guess.

## SOP 2: Loading by weight
**Building pallets**
- Heaviest boxes on the bottom, lightest on top.
- Keep the pallet square, no boxes hanging over the edge.
- Max **2,000 lb** and **60 inches tall** per pallet unless the customer says otherwise.
- Wrap 3 times at the bottom, tie the wrap into the pallet, wrap to the top.

**Loading a trailer**
- Heaviest pallets go on the floor first, **centered over the axles** (middle of the trailer), not all in the nose or the tail.
- Balance left and right: same weight on both sides.
- Only stack light pallets on top of heavy ones, never heavy on light.
- Leave no big gaps: fill with airbags or load bars so nothing shifts.
- Legal limit: **80,000 lb gross** for the whole truck (about 45,000 lb of freight on a normal 53'). If the load is close, ask the driver to scale it.
- Photo of the load before the doors close, and photo of the seal.

## SOP 3: Group chats
- Everyone on the crew is in **every** work chat.
- Reply within **15 minutes** during your shift, even just "Got it" or "On it".
- Every truck gets one message when it **arrives**, and one when it's **done**:
  - "IN: [company], trailer [#], [pallets], door [#]"
  - "DONE: [company], trailer [#], [pallets], invoiced $[amount]" + photos
- Problems (damage, short count, wrong freight): post photos right away and tag the shift lead.
- Work only. No jokes, memes or personal stuff in work chats.
- Never promise a price or a time in the chat that isn't approved.

## SOP 4: First thing in the morning
1. Unlock, lights on, check the building: anything broken, wet, or out of place since last night?
2. **Forklift check** (OSHA requires a check every shift): tires, forks, chains, horn, lights, brakes, battery or propane, no leaks. Write it on the forklift checklist. If something's wrong, don't use it, tell the shift lead.
3. Charge phones/iPad, open QuickBooks and the group chats.
4. Read the group chat from overnight. Who's coming today?
5. Walk the dock: doors clear, pallets stacked, wrap, labels and pallet jacks ready.
6. Post in the group chat: "OPEN: [name], [number] trucks expected today."

## SOP 5: Last thing at night
1. Every truck done today has an invoice sent (SOP 1).
2. Freight staying overnight: counted, wrapped, labeled with customer name and date.
3. 15-minute sweep of the dock and the aisles.
4. Forklifts parked, forks down, keys out, charging or propane off.
5. Trash out, dock doors closed and locked, lights off, alarm on.
6. **End-of-day text to Nick:**
   "END OF DAY [date]: Trucks: [#]. Invoiced: $[total]. Paid today: $[total]. Problems: [none / what happened]. Tomorrow: [# trucks expected]."

## SOP 6: Cleaning schedule
Monday and Tuesday are busy, so deep cleaning happens Wednesday–Friday.

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Every day (closing) | 15-minute sweep, trash out, pallets stacked |
| Wednesday | Sweep and clear all dock doors and plates, empty wrap/strap scraps |
| Thursday | Clean bathroom and break room, restock supplies (wrap, labels, gloves) |
| Friday | Forklifts wiped down and checked, organize empty pallets, walk the whole building for hazards |
| 1st Friday of the month | Deep clean: under racks, corners, outside the building |

Put a sign-off sheet on the wall: name and date for each task.
