## SOP 7: Receiving a truck
1. **Check the driver in**: name, company, truck and trailer number. Write it on the log.
2. **Get the BOL** and match it to the customer. Don't know the customer or the load? Call the shift lead before unloading.
3. **Check the seal** (containers and sealed trailers): seal number must match the BOL. Photo of the seal. Seal broken or wrong number? **Don't open.** Call the shift lead.
4. **Photo the load** with doors open, before you touch anything.
5. **Unload and count** every pallet or carton.
6. **Look for damage**: crushed, wet, leaning, torn wrap. Photo every bad pallet.
7. **Write problems on the BOL before you sign**: "2 pallets damaged, photos taken" or "Received 24 of 26". Never sign a BOL clean if something is wrong.
8. Driver left before you counted? Write **"Subject to count"** on the BOL.
9. **Label every pallet**: customer, date, number of pallets (example: "ACME 10/20 1 of 24").
10. Put it in its lane or rack spot and write the location down.
11. Post in the group chat: `IN: [customer], trailer [#], [pallets], door [#]` + photos.
12. Invoice (SOP 1).

## SOP 8: Releasing freight / shipping
1. **No pickup number, no freight.** The customer must send the release in writing (email or text) with carrier name and pickup number.
2. **Check the driver**: carrier name and MC/DOT match the release. Photo of driver's license and truck/trailer number.
3. Carrier name doesn't match? **Stop. Call the customer** at the number on file, not the number the driver gives you. (Fake pickups are how freight gets stolen.)
4. Pull the right pallets. Double-check the count and labels against the release.
5. Load by weight (SOP 2). Photo the loaded trailer.
6. **Seal** the trailer if the customer asks. Write the seal number on the BOL and take a photo.
7. Driver signs the BOL. Keep a copy.
8. Update the computer: shipped, date, pallets.
9. Post in the group chat: `DONE: [customer], trailer [#], [pallets]` + photos.

## SOP 9: Damage, shortage, overage (OS&D)
1. **Photos first**: close-up and the whole pallet with the label showing.
2. Write it on the BOL (SOP 7 step 7).
3. Post it in the group chat and tag the shift lead **right away**.
4. Shift lead emails the customer **the same day**: what, how many, photos.
5. **Damaged by us?** Tell the shift lead and Nick the same day. Don't hide it. Fill out an incident note: what, when, who, photos.
6. Set damaged freight aside in the **OS&D area**, labeled "HOLD – DAMAGE". Don't ship it until the customer says so.
7. **Overage** (extra freight): label "HOLD – OVERAGE", tell the customer, don't ship it.

## SOP 10: Storage + inventory
1. Every storage pallet has a **label** (customer, date in, pallet #) and a **location** (lane or rack number).
2. Location written in the computer the same day.
3. **Customer freight never mixes**: one customer per lane or rack spot.
4. **Monthly count**, 1st Friday: count every pallet, check it against the computer, report any difference to Nick.
5. **Storage billing**: per the rate sheet. Pallets in storage on billing day = what gets billed.
6. Nothing leaves storage without SOP 8.
7. Unlabeled freight found? Label it "UNKNOWN + date" and tell the shift lead the same day.

## SOP 11: Safety + forklifts
1. **Only trained, certified drivers** run a forklift. Training is on file and redone every 3 years, or after an accident or near-miss.
2. **Check the forklift before first use every shift**: horn, brakes, lights, forks, chains, tires, leaks, seatbelt. Something wrong? **Tag it "DO NOT USE"** and tell the shift lead.
3. Seatbelt on. Forks low when driving. Horn at corners and doors.
4. **Trailer safety**: wheels chocked or dock lock on, dock plate secure, before any forklift enters a trailer.
5. Safety vests on the dock. No phones while driving a forklift.
6. Nobody walks under raised forks.
7. **Anyone hurt**: first aid / call 911 → call Nick → incident note the same day.
   - Serious injury (hospital stay, amputation, eye loss): Nick reports to OSHA within **24 hours**.
   - Death: report within **8 hours**.
8. First aid kit and fire extinguishers checked monthly (Friday cleaning walk).

## SOP 12: New lead + new customer
**When someone asks for a quote**
1. Answer within **1 hour**, any time of day.
2. Ask:
   - What freight
   - How many pallets or containers
   - How often
   - Start date
   - Any special rules
3. Price from the **rate sheet only**. Anything not on it goes to Nick.
4. Send the rate sheet or quote by email. Add the lead to HubSpot.
5. Follow up in 2 days if no answer.

**When they say yes**
1. Send the **vendor packet** (COI, W-9, ACH form) if they ask for it.
2. They fill out the **new customer form**. Net 30 customers also fill out the **credit application**. Everyone else: first 3 loads paid by ACH or card.
3. Storage customers sign the **storage agreement**.
4. Set them up in QuickBooks and the customer portal.
5. Add them to a group chat with the crew. Post their special rules.
6. After the first load: Nick calls them. "How did we do?"

## SOP 13: Security
1. Doors closed and locked when no one is on the dock.
2. **Visitors and drivers sign in**: name, company, time in and out.
3. Drivers stay in the driver area unless the crew walks them to the dock.
4. Cameras on and recording. Nick checks them weekly.
5. Keys and forklift keys in the lockbox, never left in a forklift.
6. Never give out customer names, freight info or pallet counts to anyone who calls. Send them to Nick.
