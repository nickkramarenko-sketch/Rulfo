# PREP: Be Fully Set Up Before Any Outreach

Big goal: $10M a year in warehouse revenue in 3 years, Reno + Chicago (see `plan/3-year-goal.md`). Prep is step one.

Rule: **no outreach until Prep is done.** No calls, cold email or ads. When a new customer looks us up, everything should look legit, and when they say yes, the crew already knows exactly what to do.

Who: **Claude** = Claude writes or builds it. **Nick** = Nick does it (accounts, money, signatures).

---

## 1. Look legit online
- [ ] **Business email on our own domain**, e.g. nick@24hrcrossdock.com, not Gmail. Google Workspace ~$7–14/user/mo. (Nick sets up, Claude walks through)
- [ ] **One phone number everywhere**: 916-616-3481, with a professional voicemail greeting. (Claude writes greeting)
- [ ] **Website live** with real dock photos, services, quote form, "insured" and "open 24/7". (Claude built `site/`, Nick deploys)
- [ ] **Google Business Profile** claimed: hours, categories, 10+ photos, services, description. (Nick claims, Claude writes text)
- [ ] **Same name, address, phone** on every listing: Google, Bing, Apple Maps, Yelp, Warehouse Exchange, Waredock, Thomasnet. (Nick, 1 hour; Claude writes the descriptions)
- [ ] **LinkedIn**: company page + Nick's profile says "Owner, 24 HR Crossdock". (Claude writes text)
- [ ] **Photos and a 60-second dock video**: doors, forklifts, racks, clean floor, crew in vests. (Nick shoots on phone)
- [ ] **Reviews**: get the review link ready. Asking customers waits until Prep is done.

## 2. Paperwork packet (what brokers and customers ask for)
- [ ] **Certificate of insurance (COI)**. Call your agent and confirm you have: general liability, warehouse legal liability, auto (for delivery) and workers' comp. Ask for a COI you can send same day. (Nick)
- [ ] **W-9** filled in and saved as PDF. (Nick)
- [ ] **ACH / bank form** for getting paid. (Nick)
- [ ] **Vendor packet**: COI + W-9 + ACH + rate sheet in one PDF, ready to email. (Claude assembles once Nick has the files)
- [ ] **Rate sheet approved.** (Nick + Claude, 15 min)
- [ ] **Company one-pager**: who we are, services, photos, locations, contacts. (Claude)
- [ ] **Credit application** for Net 30 customers. (Claude)
- [ ] **Storage agreement + warehouse receipt terms** (sets liability limit, payment, lien rights). Claude drafts; **lawyer or insurance agent reviews before use**.
- [ ] **New customer form**: company, contacts, billing email, freight type, pallets/month, special rules. (Claude)
- [ ] **Delivery trucks**: check whether they need a USDOT number (for-hire trucks over 10,001 lb usually do). (Nick asks insurance agent)

## 5. Certifications (start the easy ones)
- [ ] **OSHA forklift training on file** for every driver (required by law; retrain every 3 years). (Nick, ~$50–100 per person online + hands-on test by Nick)

- [ ] **EPA SmartWay** partner (free, big shippers like it). (Claude walks through)
- Later, when a customer needs it: bonded warehouse (US Customs), C-TPAT, FDA food registration, ISO 9001.
