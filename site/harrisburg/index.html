Why Harrisburg

Middletown sits next to Harrisburg International Airport, where I-76 (the PA Turnpike), I-283, I-83 and I-81 come together. From here a truck reaches Philadelphia, Baltimore or Washington, DC in about 2 hours and New York or North Jersey in about 3.

That puts three port areas within a short haul: Baltimore and Philadelphia at about 2 hours, NY/NJ at about 3. West Coast freight goes through our Sacramento and Reno–Sparks docks.
