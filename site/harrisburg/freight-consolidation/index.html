How freight consolidation works in Harrisburg

Consolidation here is paired with rework. Freight arriving from several sources rarely shows up uniform: some pallets lean, some carry mixed SKUs, some need new labels. Each piece is checked at the door, and anything that would hurt the finished load is fixed before it joins it.

The load is staged on our floor and held until the last PO is in. Then it is counted, photographed and released to your carrier in one piece.

  • InboundNortheast vendors, port containers, or pickups on our own trucks within 150 miles
  • Check-inEach piece counted against its paperwork and photographed
  • Fix firstLeaning, crushed or mixed pallets rebuilt, with your approval before any charge
  • HoldOn the floor or in storage, about 500 pallets of space, until the load is complete
  • BuildSorted by stop, wrapped, labeled and photographed
  • ReleaseHanded to your carrier as one counted load

Why Harrisburg for freight consolidation

The East Coast's freight comes from many directions at once. Middletown is where they cross: I-83 from Baltimore, I-76 from Philadelphia, I-81 from north and south, and the ports of Baltimore and Philadelphia about 2 hours away, NY/NJ about 3.

Brokers use the dock to turn scattered LTL pickups into one truckload. Importers combine container freight from more than one port. Distributors gather several vendors' orders into one delivery for a single receiver.

  • PortsBaltimore and Philadelphia about 2 hrs, NY/NJ about 3 hrs
  • InterstatesI-76 (PA Turnpike), I-283, I-83 and I-81
  • Local reachOur trucks within 150 miles for pickups and final stops
  • HarrisburgAbout 10 minutes from the dock

Harrisburg consolidation questions

Building a load? Call 916-616-3481, day or night.

Can you combine port freight with domestic shipments?

Yes. Container freight from Baltimore, Philadelphia or NY/NJ and pallets from domestic shippers can go on the same outbound load.

What if a shipment arrives mixed or damaged?

We photograph it and send you the price to fix it. Once you approve, it is rebuilt, sorted or relabeled before it goes on the load. Nothing extra is charged without your OK.

How long can you hold pieces for the load?

Pieces wait on the floor while the load comes together. If that takes a while, they move into storage, billed by the day, week or month, with room for about 500 pallets.

Is there a minimum?

No. One pallet or a full truckload's worth of pieces.
