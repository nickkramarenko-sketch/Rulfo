How freight rework works in Harrisburg

Rework starts at the door. When a trailer is opened and a pallet is leaning, crushed or built from the wrong SKUs, we stop, photograph it and tell you what it will take to fix. You approve the work before we charge for it.

Then the pallet is rebuilt on our floor and photographed again, so you have proof of how it arrived and how it left. Once it is right, it goes back into the flow: cross-docked, added to a consolidated load, stored or delivered.

  • Photos firstCondition recorded at the door and sent to you with a price to fix
  • RebuildLeaning or crushed pallets restacked onto sound pallets
  • SortMixed SKUs separated and built into clean pallets by SKU or PO
  • RelabelNew labels for the next carrier or the receiver
  • SecureShrink wrap, corner boards and banding
  • Photos afterThe finished pallet, before it leaves our floor

Why Harrisburg for freight rework

Freight rework is one of the main jobs at this dock. Pallets that come off a ship at Baltimore or Philadelphia (about 2 hours) or NY/NJ (about 3 hours), or off a long cross-country run, can shift on the way. Fixing them in Middletown keeps the problem from reaching a Northeast receiver.

Once the freight is clean, it is close to the market: I-76, I-83, I-81 and I-283 are at hand, and our trucks deliver within 150 miles.

  • Freight brokersA load the receiver may refuse, fixed and moving again
  • ImportersMixed-SKU containers sorted into pallets the receiver can take
  • DistributorsPallets rebuilt and relabeled to a customer's spec
  • CarriersShifted freight made safe before the next leg

Harrisburg freight rework questions

Got a pallet that needs help? Call 916-616-3481, any hour.

What does rework cost?

It depends on the job. You get photos and a price before we start, and nothing extra is charged until you approve it.

Will I see the freight before and after?

Yes. Every reworked pallet is photographed when it arrives and again when it is finished.

Can you fix freight that is not already at your dock?

Yes. Our trucks pick up within 150 miles of Middletown, one-time or recurring. We rework it here and redeliver it or send it on with your carrier.

What happens after the rework?

Whatever the freight needs next: reloaded onto an outbound truck, added to a consolidated load, stored by the day, week or month, or delivered.

Do your other docks rework freight?

Yes. All three docks offer freight rework; it is a specialty here and in Sacramento.
