How transloading works

Send the packing list before the box arrives and it turns faster. Every carton is counted against your paperwork.

  1. 1

    Book it

    Send the container or trailer number, the ETA, the packing list and how you want it palletized.

  2. 2

    Unload

    Floor-loaded freight is unloaded by hand; palletized freight comes off by forklift. Seal checked and photos taken first.

  3. 3

    Palletize

    Cartons built onto pallets, sorted by SKU or PO, stretch-wrapped and labeled.

  4. 4

    Count and report

    Counted against the packing list. Overages, shortages and damage photographed and reported the same day.

  5. 5

    Reload or store

    Onto your outbound truck, into storage until you're ready, or a mix of both.

Why transload with us

A container that sits at the port costs you every day. A dock that can unload it, build clean pallets and put them on a domestic trailer, or hold them, keeps the freight moving.

Because we also cross-dock, store and deliver, one call covers the whole move: unload, palletize, store, then deliver within the dock's radius on our own trucks.

  • Equipment20' and 40' containers and 53' trailers, floor-loaded or palletized
  • PalletsBuilt, wrapped and labeled to your spec or the receiver's
  • OS&DPhotographed and reported the same day
  • StorageDaily, weekly, monthly or long-term. See warehousing and 3PL
  • DeliveryOur own trucks, within each dock's radius. See pickups and deliveries
  • PricingQuoted per container; pallets and wrap itemized, no hidden fees

Transloading questions

Don't see yours? Call 916-616-3481, any time.

Which dock is closest to the ports?

Sacramento is about an hour from the Port of Stockton and about two hours from the Port of Oakland. Harrisburg is about two hours from the ports of Baltimore and Philadelphia and about three hours from NY/NJ. Reno–Sparks is about four hours from the Bay Area over I-80.

Do you unload floor-loaded containers?

Yes. Floor-loaded freight is unloaded by hand, then palletized and stretch-wrapped. Tell us how you want it built and we build it that way.

Can the freight wait after unloading?

Yes. Store it with us by the day, week or month and release it when you're ready, in full or in part.

How is transloading priced?

Per container, based on what's in it and how it's loaded. Pallets, wrap and labels are itemized. Call for a quote; we call back within an hour.
