Why keep inventory in Reno

Reno sits on I-80, the main freight lane between Northern California and the rest of the country. Freight staged here reaches Sacramento the same morning and most of the western U.S. within one day.

Nevada has no corporate income tax, and its Freeport law exempts inventory stored here and shipped out of state from inventory tax.

Why sellers need a prep partner now

As of January 1, 2026, Amazon no longer preps or labels FBA inventory in the U.S. Every unit has to arrive at Amazon fully prepped and labeled by the seller or a prep center. That's the work we do.

Store your bulk inventory with us in Reno, and we send prepped cases into Amazon as your stock runs low, so you aren't paying Amazon's storage rates for months of inventory.

  • FNSKU labelingCovering old barcodes, one label per unit
  • Poly baggingSuffocation-warning bags for apparel and loose items
  • Bubble wrapFragile and glass items protected to spec
  • BundlingSets bagged or shrink-wrapped and marked as one unit
  • Case packingBoxed and labeled within Amazon's size and weight limits
  • InspectionDamaged or wrong units pulled and reported before shipping

Common questions

Don't see yours? Call 916-616-3481.

How is storage billed?

Per pallet, billed weekly, plus a handling charge when freight comes in and goes out. Every account is quoted on its own volume.

Is there a minimum?

No hard minimum. Our best fit is projects from 100 to 5,000 pallets, but call us with whatever you have.

Do I need a long-term contract?

No. Storage is billed weekly and we'll talk through terms that fit your volume.

How fast is my inventory available after it arrives?

Same day. Freight is received, counted against your ASN and BOL, photographed and posted to your inventory the day it arrives.

What happens if freight arrives damaged?

We photograph it at the door, note it on the paperwork and tell you the same day. We can restack or rewrap it with your approval.

Do you work weekends or after hours?

Yes, by appointment. Set it up with us before 10pm.

Is my inventory insured?

We're fully insured and send a certificate of insurance on request. We recommend you also keep your own cargo coverage.

I'm not in Nevada. Can I still use you?

Yes. Most of our customers ship from outside Nevada, including Chicago, Atlanta, Tennessee, Pennsylvania, Utah and California.
