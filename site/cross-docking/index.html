How our cross-dock works

The same five steps on every load, at every dock, with the name of the person who handled it.

  1. 1

    Book the door

    Any day and time, in the client portal or by phone. Send the loads, pallet counts and where each one goes.

  2. 2

    Check in

    The driver checks in at our kiosk in their own language and gets a door or a place in line. Drivers can read the steps first on our check-in page.

  3. 3

    Unload and count

    Seal checked, every pallet counted against the BOL and photographed at the door. Unloaded within 2 hours of check-in, during dock hours.

  4. 4

    Sort

    Freight is sorted by destination and staged by outbound truck. Partial loads wait on the floor until their truck arrives.

  5. 5

    Reload

    Loaded on the outbound truck the same day, photographed and released with signed paperwork. Securement checked and signed before it leaves.

What you get on every load

A cross-dock is only as good as its count. If something is short, over or damaged, you hear it from us the same day, with photos, before the receiver calls you.

Freight that can't go out today doesn't get lost on the floor. It waits in its lane, or moves into storage, billed weekly.

Cross-docking questions

Don't see yours? Call 916-616-3481, any time.

How fast is my trailer unloaded?

Unloaded within 2 hours of check-in, during dock hours. During dock hours, Mon–Fri 6am–10pm. The clock starts when your driver checks in.

Is it really reloaded the same day?

Yes, when the outbound truck is here the same day. If it isn't, the freight waits on the floor in its own lane until it is, and longer stays move to storage, billed weekly.

What happens if freight arrives short or damaged?

It's photographed at the door, noted on the paperwork and reported to you the same day. We can rebuild or rewrap it, but nothing extra is charged until you approve it.

Can you cross-dock after hours or on weekends?

Yes, by appointment. Docks open Mon–Fri 6am–10pm; nights and weekends by appointment; after-hours work carries a $100 fee. The phone is answered 24/7, so call to set it up.

What freight can you cross-dock?

Dry freight only: standard pallets, drums, crates, oversized pieces, loose cargo and mixed loads. Our forklifts are rated to 4,500 lb, so call first for heavier pieces, and call first for hazmat or other regulated freight.
