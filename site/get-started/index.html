Onboarding, step by step

Storage-only accounts can be live within days. Fulfillment accounts usually take two to four weeks.

  1. 1

    RFQ and rate quote

    Send commodity, pallet count and dimensions, SKU count, monthly turns and services needed. We call you back within one hour and send written rates.

  2. 2

    Agreement and account setup

    Sign the warehousing service agreement, send your COI and billing contact, and complete a credit application for net terms.

  3. 3

    SKU master and integration

    Send your item master with dimensions, weights and units per pallet. If you need EDI, an API or a store connection, we set it up and test it with you.

  4. 4

    First inbound

    Send an ASN, label your freight and book a dock appointment. We receive, reconcile against the ASN and BOL, and report any OS&D.

  5. 5

    Go live

    Inventory is posted and available. Release orders ship with BOL, seal record, photos and POD.

Sending freight to us

Following these keeps receiving fast and your counts accurate.

Before it ships

  • Send an advance shipment notice (ASN) at least 24 hours ahead, listing SKUs, quantities and PO numbers
  • Book a delivery appointment by phone or email
  • Put your company name and reference number on the BOL

Pallets and cartons

  • Standard 48" × 40" pallets, stretch-wrapped
  • One SKU per pallet where possible
  • Each carton labeled with SKU and quantity
  • Oversize pallets and crates accepted, just tell us first

Common questions

Don't see yours? Call 916-616-3481.

How is storage billed?

Per pallet, billed weekly, plus a handling charge when freight comes in and goes out. Every account is quoted on its own volume.

Is there a minimum?

No. One pallet or a thousand, call us with whatever you have.

Do I need a long-term contract?

No. Storage is billed weekly and we'll talk through terms that fit your volume.

How fast is my inventory available after it arrives?

Same day. Freight is received, counted against your ASN and BOL, photographed and posted to your inventory the day it arrives.

What happens if freight arrives damaged?

We photograph it at the door, note it on the paperwork and tell you the same day. We can restack or rewrap it with your approval.

Do you work weekends or after hours?

Yes, by appointment. Set it up with us before 10pm.

Is my inventory insured?

We're fully insured and send a certificate of insurance on request. We recommend you also keep your own cargo coverage.

I'm not in Nevada. Can I still use you?

Yes. Most of our customers ship from outside Nevada, including Chicago, Atlanta, Tennessee, Pennsylvania, Utah and California.

Need a dock?

Call 24/7, or send four quick fields and we call you back within one hour.
