Sending freight to us

Following these keeps receiving fast and your counts accurate. Not sure which dock? Compare locations.

Before it ships

  • Send the BOL or an advance shipment notice with quantities and PO numbers
  • Book the truck in the client portal, or by phone or email
  • Put your company name and reference number on the BOL

Freight

  • Dry freight only: standard pallets, drums, crates, oversized pieces, loose cargo and mixed loads
  • Standard 48" × 40" pallets, stretch-wrapped, where you can
  • Forklifts rated to 4,500 lb: call first for heavier pieces
  • Hazmat or other regulated freight: call first

Common questions

Don't see yours? Call 916-616-3481.

Is there a minimum?

No. One pallet or a thousand, call us with whatever you have.

Do I need a long-term contract?

No. Storage is billed weekly and we'll talk through terms that fit your volume. Long-term contract warehousing is available if you want it.

How soon do I see what was received?

The same day: counts and photos for every load in the client portal.

What happens if freight arrives damaged?

We photograph it at the door, note it on the paperwork and tell you the same day. We can restack or rewrap it with your approval.

How fast do you unload?

Unloaded within 2 hours of check-in, during dock hours (Mon–Fri 6am–10pm). The clock starts when your driver checks in.

Do you work weekends or after hours?

Yes. Docks open Mon–Fri 6am–10pm; nights and weekends by appointment, $100 fee for 10pm–6am. Call 916-616-3481 any time to set it up.

Is my freight insured?

We're fully insured and send a certificate of insurance on request. We recommend you also keep your own cargo coverage.

Need a dock?

Call 24/7, or send four quick fields and we call you back within one hour.
