How cross-docking works in Sacramento

A typical trailer here comes in on I-80 or I-5 carrying freight for several Northern California receivers. We break it down on the floor by destination and load each lane onto its outbound truck the same day.

With 8 doors and 40,000 sq ft, inbound and outbound trailers work side by side. Freight that is not ready to leave waits on the floor or moves into storage until its truck shows up.

  • UnloadUnloaded within 2 hours of check-in, during dock hours

Why Sacramento for cross-docking

North Highlands sits next to McClellan Park, with I-80, Business 80 and I-5 close by. Linehaul from the east on I-80 and freight on the I-5 lane both reach the door without a detour, and Sacramento, Stockton and the Bay Area are short runs out.

Brokers use the dock when a load covers several Sacramento and Bay Area receivers. Carriers drop a linehaul trailer and pick up local loads. Importers send drayed containers that need to be split before final delivery.

Sacramento cross-docking questions

Anything else, call 916-616-3481, day or night.

How fast does a trailer turn in Sacramento?

Freight is reloaded onto outbound trucks the same day during dock hours, Mon–Fri 6am–10pm. After hours: nights and weekends by appointment; after-hours work carries a $100 fee.

Can part of a load wait for its truck?

Yes. Partial loads wait on the floor. If a receiver needs more time, the pallets move into storage, billed by the day, week or month, with room for 500–1,000 pallets.

Can you deliver the last leg yourselves?

Yes, within 150 miles of North Highlands, on our own trucks with our own drivers, one-time or on a recurring schedule. You get signed proof of delivery.

What if a pallet arrives leaning or crushed?

We photograph it at the door and tell you the same day. We can rebuild it here as freight rework, and nothing extra is charged until you approve it.

What freight can you cross-dock here?

Dry freight only: standard pallets, drums, crates, oversized pieces, loose cargo and mixed loads. Our forklifts are rated to 4,500 lb. For heavier pieces or regulated freight, call first.
