How transloading works in Sacramento

Your drayman brings the container to one of our 8 doors. Floor-loaded cartons come off by hand and are built onto pallets; palletized freight comes off by forklift. Every carton is checked against your packing list before anything is reloaded.

From there each pallet goes one of two ways: onto an outbound 53' trailer, or into storage on our floor until you call for it. Many loads do a bit of both.

  • What arrives20' and 40' containers and 53' trailers, floor-loaded or palletized
  • UnloadCartons counted against the packing list; forklifts rated to 4,500 lb
  • PalletizeBuilt by SKU or PO, stretch-wrapped and labeled for the next carrier
  • OS&DShortages, overages and damage photographed and reported the same day
  • ReloadOnto your outbound truck, or onto ours for delivery within 150 miles
  • Or storeDaily, weekly or monthly, with room for 500–1,000 pallets

Why Sacramento for transloading

Two ports are within a short dray. Oakland containers reach North Highlands in about 2 hours over I-80; Stockton boxes come up I-5 in about 1 hour. Transloading here gets the freight off the container and into domestic trailers close to Sacramento receivers, instead of at the port.

Our Reno–Sparks dock does the same work about 2 hours up I-80, so freight bound for Nevada or points east can be transloaded on either side of the Sierra.

  • ImportersOne container split across several Sacramento and Bay Area customers, or part of it held
  • Freight brokersA floor-loaded box palletized before the receiver will take it
  • DistributorsPort freight moved into 53' trailers for inland lanes on I-80 and I-5

Sacramento transloading questions

Have a container on the water? Call 916-616-3481 and we will plan it with you.

Do you unload floor-loaded containers?

Yes. Floor-loaded cartons are unloaded by hand, built onto pallets by SKU or PO and stretch-wrapped. Palletized containers come off by forklift.

What do you need before the container arrives?

The packing list with SKUs, carton counts and POs, the container number and ETA, how you want it palletized, and whether it reloads, stays in storage or both.

How is transloading priced?

Quoted per container, based on the carton count and how the freight has to be palletized. Pallets and wrap are itemized on the quote.

Can part of the container stay with you?

Yes. Reload what is ready and keep the rest in storage by the day, week or month. Release it to any carrier, or we deliver it within 150 miles of North Highlands.

Is there anything you can't take?

We handle dry freight only, with no temperature control. Pieces over 4,500 lb and hazmat or other regulated freight: call first.
