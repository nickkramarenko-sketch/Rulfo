Why Sacramento

The dock sits in North Highlands with I-80, Business 80 and I-5 close by, so freight moving in and out of Northern California passes our door. Downtown Sacramento is about 15 minutes away.

Containers come up from the Port of Oakland in about 2 hours and from the Port of Stockton in about 1 hour. Freight headed east can go over I-80 to our Reno–Sparks dock, about 2 hours away. East Coast freight runs through Harrisburg, PA.
