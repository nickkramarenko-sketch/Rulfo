Every truck, six steps

From booking to done, here's what happens to each truck you send us.

  1. 1

    Booked

    Pick any day and time. Add every load: PO, BOL, pallets, weight and notes.

  2. 2

    On the way

    Call or text us the ETA. Or your driver updates it from a link.Coming soon

  3. 3

    Checked in

    Your driver checks in at our kiosk in their own language and gets a door or a place in line.

  4. 4

    Waiting

    Waiting trucks line up in check-in order. Our 2-hour promise counts from check-in.

  5. 5

    At the door

    Seal checked, counted against the BOL and photographed. Damage gets photos and a price to fix.

  6. 6

    Done

    Counts and photos on file. Outbound loads are secured and signed before they leave.

Portal questions

Don't see yours? Call 916-616-3481, 24/7.

Who gets a login?

Every customer. Open an account in the portal, or call 916-616-3481 and we set it up with you.

Does one login cover all three docks?

Yes. Sparks, North Highlands and Middletown, in one place.

What is the 2-hour promise?

Unloaded within 2 hours of check-in, during dock hours (Mon–Fri 6am–10pm). The clock starts when your driver checks in at our kiosk.

How does my driver check in?

At our kiosk, in English, Spanish, Russian, Ukrainian, Punjabi or Hindi. They type the 4-digit code from dispatch or find your company, confirm their name and cell, and take a photo of the paperwork. Send them our driver check-in page.

Do you text my driver the door number?

Coming soon Not yet. Your driver gets the door number, or a place in line, at check-in. Door texts are coming soon.

What happens when freight is damaged?

We photograph it when we find it and send you a price to fix. Accept, decline or call us. Nothing is charged until you approve it.

What is a securement check?

Before an outbound load leaves, the loader records the bars, straps, plywood and airbags (ours or the driver's), the gear tag numbers and the seal, takes five photos, rates the load 1 to 5 and signs it.

What does the securement plan cost?

Call for a quote: 916-616-3481.

Can I get alerts by text or email?

Coming soon Not yet. Soon you'll pick text, email or both for each person and each event. Until then, call us any time at 916-616-3481.

Is there a weekly report?

Coming soon Coming soon: one short email every Friday with your week: trucks, pallets in and out, wait times, the 2-hour promise and damage.

Can other customers see my freight?

No. Other customers never see your trucks, your loads or your company name.
