Freight rework and restacking.

Freight rework at all three docks, and a specialty in Harrisburg. Leaning, crushed or mixed pallets are rebuilt, sorted, relabeled and wrapped so the next carrier or the receiver takes them without a fight. Photos before and after, and nothing extra is charged until you approve it.

How rework works

You decide before we touch it. That's the rule at every dock.

  1. 1

    Found at the door

    A leaning, crushed or mixed pallet is photographed the moment it's unloaded and noted on the paperwork.

  2. 2

    You approve

    We tell you what it needs and what it costs. Nothing extra is charged until you say yes.

  3. 3

    Rebuild

    Re-palletized, restacked or sorted by SKU onto new pallets, with damaged cartons set aside and counted.

  4. 4

    Wrap and label

    Shrink wrap, corner boards and banding as the load needs, and new labels where the old ones are gone or wrong.

  5. 5

    Photos after

    Photographed again and released to the next truck, stored, or delivered on our own trucks.

What we fix

Freight that shifted in transit, pallets built badly at origin, mixed SKUs that a receiver will refuse. Rework turns a problem load into one that ships.

Because it's done at a cross-dock, the rebuilt freight can go straight back out on an outbound truck, often the same day it arrived.

  • Leaning palletsRestacked square and rewrapped
  • Crushed or broken palletsFreight moved onto new pallets and rebuilt
  • Mixed SKUsSorted by SKU, PO or receiver onto separate pallets
  • RelabelingNew pallet or carton labels to the receiver's rules
  • SecuringShrink wrap, corner boards and banding
  • ProofPhotos before and after, labor itemized on the invoice

Freight rework questions

Don't see yours? Call 916-616-3481, any time.

Will you rework freight without asking me?

No. We photograph the problem, tell you what it needs and what it costs, and wait for your approval. Nothing extra is charged until you approve it.

Which dock does the most rework?

Freight rework is a specialty at our Harrisburg dock, with 30 dock doors, and at Sacramento. Reno–Sparks does it too, on the same terms.

Can I send a load to you only for rework?

Yes. Send the trailer in, we rebuild what needs it, and it goes back out on the same trailer or a new one, or into storage until you're ready.

How is rework billed?

Quoted first, itemized on the invoice. No minimums.
