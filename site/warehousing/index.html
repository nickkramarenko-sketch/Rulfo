How storage works

Simple, and the same at every dock.

  1. 1

    Quote

    Tell us the freight, the pallet count and the term. We call back within an hour with rates.

  2. 2

    Receive

    Counted against the BOL, photographed and given a location the day it arrives.

  3. 3

    Store

    Floor or racked, one customer's freight per lane. Billed weekly.

  4. 4

    Release

    Full or partial releases to your carrier, or delivered by our own trucks.

Built to handle more than standard pallets

Brokers, carriers, manufacturers, distributors and shippers send us whatever the load holds. One dock that handles all of it means fewer handoffs and fewer calls.

Standard pallets

Wrapped or loose, single or stacked, counted against the BOL and photographed at the door.

Drums

Drums on pallets or loose, banded and strapped for the next leg. Regulated contents: call first.

Crates

Wooden crates and boxed machinery, moved by forklift and photographed on arrival.

Oversized freight

Long, wide or awkward pieces. Forklifts rated to 4,500 lb; heavier pieces, call first.

Loose cargo

Floor-loaded cartons unloaded by hand, palletized, wrapped and labeled.

Mixed freight

Mixed SKUs and pallet sizes sorted by PO, destination or stop.

Warehousing questions

Don't see yours? Call 916-616-3481, any time.

Is there a minimum or a long contract?

No. One pallet is fine, and storage is billed weekly. Long-term contract warehousing is there if you want it, not as a condition.

Which dock has the most space?

Reno–Sparks, with room for 1,000–3,000 pallets. Sacramento holds 500–1,000 and Harrisburg about 500.

Do you offer fulfillment?

Our Reno–Sparks dock offers fulfillment and Amazon FBA and Walmart prep. Pallet storage and contract warehousing are available at all three docks.

Can stored freight be picked up and delivered by you?

Yes, on our own trucks, one-time or on a schedule, within 150 miles of Sacramento and Harrisburg and 100 miles of Reno–Sparks.
