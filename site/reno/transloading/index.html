How transloading works in Reno

Book the container, send the packing list, and we take it from there. Every carton is counted against your paperwork, and anything short, over or damaged is photographed and reported the same day.

Floor-loaded boxes come off by hand and are built onto pallets. Palletized freight comes off by forklift, rated to 4,500 lb. Then it goes where you need it: onto the next truck, or into our warehouse.

  • DevanningFloor-loaded 20' and 40' containers and 53' trailers unloaded by hand
  • Palletized loadsPalletized containers and trailers unloaded, checked and rewrapped where needed
  • PalletizingCartons built onto pallets, sorted by SKU or PO, and stretch-wrapped
  • TransloadReloaded onto outbound trucks, often the same day
  • Put awayOr stored in our Sparks warehouse by the day, week or month until you release it
  • OS&DOverages, shortages and damage photographed and reported the same day

Why Reno for transloading

Containers from the Port of Oakland reach our Sparks dock over I‑80 in about four hours. From here, freight is about a day's drive from most of California, Oregon, Utah and Arizona, and our own trucks cover the last 100 miles around Sparks.

If the freight is staying in California, our Sacramento dock is about 2 hours from Oakland and does the same work.

  • Importers and forwardersContainer freight moved inland to Northern Nevada and stored until it sells
  • Freight brokersFloor-loaded freight palletized and wrapped for an LTL or truckload carrier
  • Shippers in the corridorContainer loads split into deliveries around USA Parkway and the Tahoe-Reno Industrial Center

Transloading in Reno: questions

Do you unload floor-loaded containers?

Yes. Floor-loaded 20' and 40' containers and 53' trailers are unloaded by hand, built onto pallets, sorted by SKU or PO and stretch-wrapped.

Can the freight go out the same day?

Often, yes. When the outbound truck is ready, the pallets are reloaded the same day. If it is not, they wait in storage, billed weekly.

Can you deliver it after unloading?

Yes, on our own trucks within 100 miles of Sparks, with signed proof of delivery. See pickups and deliveries in Reno.

Do you take refrigerated containers?

No. The Sparks dock is dry freight only, with no temperature control. Call first for hazmat or other regulated freight and for single pieces over 4,500 lb.
