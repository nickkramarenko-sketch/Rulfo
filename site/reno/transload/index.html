How it works

Book the container, send the packing list, and we take it from there. Every carton is counted against your paperwork, and anything short, over or damaged is photographed and reported the same day.

Containers from Oakland and the West Coast ports reach our Sparks dock on I-80 in about four hours.

  • DevanningFloor-loaded 20' and 40' containers and 53' trailers unloaded
  • PalletizingCartons built onto pallets, sorted by SKU or PO, and stretch-wrapped
  • TransloadReloaded onto outbound trucks, often the same day
  • Put awayOr stored in our warehouse until you're ready
  • OS&DOverages, shortages and damage photographed and reported the same day

What we need from you

The more we know before the container arrives, the faster it turns.

Before it arrives

  • Packing list with SKUs, carton counts and POs
  • Container number and ETA
  • How you want it palletized

After unloading

  • Store it with us, or
  • Reload it onto your outbound truck
  • Or a mix of both

Pricing

  • Quoted per container, based on carton count
  • Pallets and wrap itemized
  • No hidden fees
