More than a place to park pallets

Our 3PL warehousing in Reno includes the handling around your storage, with no other vendor in the middle.

We store standard pallets, drums, crates, oversized pieces, loose cargo and mixed freight. Dry freight only, no temperature control. Forklifts are rated to 4,500 lb; for heavier pieces, hazmat or other regulated freight, call first.

  • Inbound handlingTrailers and containers unloaded; floor-loaded cartons palletized and wrapped (transloading)
  • Outbound handlingReleases built, wrapped, labeled and loaded for your carrier
  • Cross-dock plus storagePart of a trailer reloads the same day and the rest is stored (cross-docking)
  • Scheduled and recurring movesOur own trucks bring freight in and take releases out within 100 miles, one-time or every week (pickups and deliveries)
  • Rework while storedLeaning pallets rebuilt, relabeled and rewrapped; nothing extra is charged until you approve it

Pallet storage in Reno: questions

Is there a minimum stay or pallet count?

No minimums. Store one pallet for a day or a truckload on a long-term contract. Storage is billed weekly.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Short-term and monthly storage need no contract. Long-term contract warehousing is there when you want a set block of space.

What can't you store?

Anything that needs temperature control: we handle dry freight only. Call first for hazmat or other regulated freight and for single pieces over 4,500 lb.
