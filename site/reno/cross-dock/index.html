How it works

East Coast consolidators send us one westbound trailer. We check the seal, unload, count against the BOL and photograph every load. Reno freight stays on our floor for delivery, California freight goes back on the same trailer in your delivery order, and anything that has to wait goes into storage.

The first 24 hours on every pallet are free before storage billing starts.

Our cross-dock process

Every load gets the same process, with the name of the person who handled it.

  1. 1

    Book the door

    Send the manifest: loads, pallet counts and where each goes.

  2. 2

    Check in

    Driver checks in at our kiosk and gets a door.

  3. 3

    Unload and count

    Seal read, counted against the BOL, photographed.

  4. 4

    Split

    Reno freight to the floor, California freight back on the trailer.

  5. 5

    Out the door

    Loaded, photographed and released with paperwork signed.
